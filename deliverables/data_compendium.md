## Counter-Argument Evidence Ratings

| Claim | Verdict | Key Evidence | Source |
|-------|---------|-------------|--------|
| "H-1B workers displace Americans" | LARGELY FALSE | No displacement found; firms hiring H-1B workers hire MORE native graduates | Richmond Fed 2025 |
| "Immigrants are a fiscal burden" | LARGELY FALSE | $14.5T fiscal surplus (1994-2023); immigrants pay 17% more in taxes per capita | Cato 2026 |
| "We can innovate without immigrants" | LARGELY FALSE | 36% of patents; 1920s restriction caused 68% invention decline | NBER, Moser & San |
| "Restricting immigration protects jobs" | LARGELY FALSE | Firms offshore 0.3-0.9 jobs per unfilled H-1B; do NOT hire more Americans | NBER/Glennon |
| "H-1B workers suppress wages" | MIXED | Firm-level suppression exists (60% below median); economy-wide positive (+1.7-2.6%) | EPI, NBER |
| "Too many immigrants lower quality" | MIXED | U.S. leads in patent importance; but volume ≠ quality | CSIS |

## Data Quality Legend

| Level | Description | Use in Visualization |
|-------|-------------|---------------------|
| High | Direct from primary sources (NFAP, AIC, USCIS) | Solid colors, full opacity |
| High (cited) | From peer-reviewed research, not independently verified | Solid colors with source attribution |
| Cited projection | Modeled/projected by cited study | Full opacity with "projection" label |
| Medium | Survey data, derived calculations, or single-source | Reduced opacity, footnote |
| Low | Small-sample, survivorship bias, rough approximation | Dotted borders, "estimated" label |
