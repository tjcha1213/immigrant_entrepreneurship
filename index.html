Act V

So What?

The cost of restriction is not hypothetical. We can model it. And history has already shown us what happens when America closes its doors.

GDP Impact: Three Scenarios (2025–2035)

Cumulative GDP change relative to baseline. Restriction costs $12.1 trillion; reform adds $456B from H-1B expansion alone. Sources: NFAP, Hoover Institution

History's Warning: The 1920s Restriction

When America restricted immigration in 1924, U.S. invention declined 30% — and the effects persisted for 40 years. Source: Stanford GSB

What's at Stake

The data is clear. Immigrant entrepreneurs are not a side note in the American innovation story — they are the story.

  • Immigrant-founded companies employ 15.4 million people and generate $8.6 trillion in revenue
  • Continued restriction could cost $12.1 trillion in GDP and 15.7 million workers over the next decade
  • The 1920s showed that closing doors reduces innovation for 40 years
  • Canada, the UK, and others are actively recruiting the talent America turns away
  • H-1B reform alone could generate +$456B in GDP and +$113B in tax revenue
